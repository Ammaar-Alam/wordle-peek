# Privacy policy

Effective October 2, 2026

Wordle Peek does not collect, sell, or share any personal data. It has no server, analytics, advertising, or accounts.

## What runs where

- **Puzzle data.** The extension runs only on Wordle pages at nytimes.com. It requests puzzle details (the answer, puzzle number, and editor) from the same nytimes.com address the game uses. No other network requests are made.
- **Your board.** Guesses are read from the page to find the words that still fit. They are not stored or sent anywhere.
- **Voice.** The microphone is used only while you hold the mic button or the space bar. Audio is processed on your device and is never recorded, stored, or transmitted. The browser asks for microphone permission on nytimes.com the first time you use it.
- **Local storage.** Panel state, the hints setting, and recent puzzle navigation are saved in the browser's storage for nytimes.com. The speech model is cached there too so it loads quickly next time.

## Removing data

Clearing site data for nytimes.com or uninstalling the extension removes everything Wordle Peek stored.
